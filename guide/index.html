The 20 keyboard shortcuts everyone should know. Copy, paste, undo, find, save, select and switch windows. They work in almost every program, on Windows and Mac.

Understanding the keyboard

Windows and Mac keyboards: which key is which. Ctrl and Command, Alt and Option, Delete and Backspace, and the everyday shortcuts side by side. For anyone who uses both, or has just switched.

The function keys F1 to F12 explained. What each usually does, and why on a laptop they may change the volume instead until you press Fn.

A task everyone needs

How to take a screenshot on Windows and Mac. Every method, which one to choose, where the picture goes, and what to check before sending it to someone.

A word on habits

Nobody learns shortcuts by reading. They are learnt by using three of them for a week until the fingers know them, and then three more. Each guide here is short enough to read in a few minutes. The learning happens afterwards, at your own keyboard, with a note stuck to the edge of the screen.
