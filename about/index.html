About Keyboard Shortcuts

Keyboard Shortcuts by ShareYourGroup lists the keyboard shortcuts of everyday programs and systems, 13 pages of them, with a search box, printable pages and 7 guides on using the keyboard well.

Where the content comes from

The wording is our own. The Windows page follows Microsoft's own list, "Keyboard shortcuts in Windows" on support.microsoft.com, read on 12 November 2026. The other pages list long-standing shortcuts from our own use of each program; they were not all re-checked against the maker's help pages on that date. Shortcuts can differ between versions, languages and keyboard layouts, and programs let people change them. Each program's Help menu has its current list.

An independent site

This is an independent reference site. It is not connected with Microsoft, Apple, Google or any other maker of the programs named here. Product names belong to their owners and are used only to say where a shortcut works. Programs change; if a shortcut does not work for you, check the program's own help.
